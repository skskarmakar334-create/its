Who we are

IT Consultants PLC. (ITC PLC.) is a public limited company and a Licensed Payment System Operator (PSO). The company was formed in 2000 and introduced its technology and business operations in 2001.

ITC provides end-to-end payment services, infrastructure and technology to banks, governments, financial institutions and non-financial organizations.

Its technology and payment solutions have been recognized, accredited and certified by international payment organizations and governing bodies.
