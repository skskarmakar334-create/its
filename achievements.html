02 // Timeline

From foundation to infrastructure.

A technology company is measured not only by where it starts, but by how continuously it evolves.

2000

ITC is formed

IT Consultants PLC. was formed as a technology company focused on developing solutions for Bangladesh's growing financial and electronic transaction environment.

2001

Operations begin

Technology and business operations were introduced, establishing the foundation for ITC's long-term payment and banking technology journey.

2008

BEPS acquisition

ITC acquired Bangladesh Electronic Payment Systems Ltd. on 20 September 2008, strengthening its position in electronic transaction processing.

Q-CASH ERA

Expanding financial connectivity

The Q-Cash ecosystem grew into a major independent ATM and merchant network, connecting member banks and supporting electronic transaction processing.

DIGITAL ERA

Beyond traditional banking

ITC expanded its technology ecosystem across e-commerce, mobile financial services, QR payments, internet banking, payment gateways and modern digital transaction infrastructure.

TODAY

Building the next transaction era

Today, ITC operates as a public limited company and licensed payment system operator, continuing to develop secure, scalable and intelligent financial infrastructure.
